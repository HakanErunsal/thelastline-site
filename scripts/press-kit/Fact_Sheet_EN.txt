THE LAST LINE — FACT SHEET (EN)

Title: The Last Line
Tagline: Hold the barricade against up to 150 zombies, scavenge ruined cities,
and face Souls-style bosses in a mobile survival shooter built for iPhone and iPad.

Short description:
The Last Line is a mobile zombie survival shooter built in Unreal Engine 5 for
iPhone and iPad. Defend barricades against massive hordes, scavenge ruined cities, upgrade
weapons, survive Endless nights, and fight arena bosses with Souls-style attack
reads — all with crowd density tuned for mobile.

Release date: 10 September 2026
Pre-order: 20 August 2026
Platform: iPhone and iPad (iOS)
Price: Free to play
Genre: Action, survival, strategy
Players: Single-player
Engine: Unreal Engine 5.7
Languages: 46 UI languages; 10 full campaign voice dubs
Developer / Publisher: Rigbak
