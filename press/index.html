Highlights

What makes it different

Hold back the horde

Up to 150 zombies attack at once with custom crowd tech tuned for iPhone. Heavies, flyers, and mixed waves keep the barricade under constant pressure without sacrificing frame rate.

Survive beyond the barricade

Defend set-piece arenas, scavenge ruined cities for resources, upgrade weapons and loadouts, and push through Endless nights when the siege never stops.

Face arena bosses

Souls-style bosses read distance, angle, and health. Dodge, stagger, and recover from tunnel vision in close third-person fights that change the pace after the horde.

About the game

The Last Line is a mobile zombie survival shooter built in Unreal Engine 5 for iPhone. Frame budget went into visible combat: massive hordes, barricade sieges in first- and third-person, city scavenging, weapon progression, Endless nights, and arena bosses with Souls-style attack reads.

Rigbak developed the game as a focused iPhone launch. Menus and story text ship in 46 languages, with full campaign voice dubs in ten languages at release.
